📄 Key Tax Documents

Easy – Must Know
00:00
Your task:

Match each document to its correct description. These are the exact forms and papers you will handle daily in this role.

Quick Reference (after you finish)

  • W-2 – Wage and tax statement from an employer (employee wages, federal/state tax withheld).
  • 1099 – Reports various non-employee income (independent contractors, interest, dividends, etc.). Common: 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-INT.
  • K-1 – Partner’s or shareholder’s share of income, deductions, credits from a partnership, S-corp, or trust.
  • Tax Organizer – Questionnaire the firm sends to the client to gather all needed information for preparing the return.
  • Engagement Letter – Contract that defines the scope of services, fees, and responsibilities between the firm and the client.
  • Extension (Form 4868) – Request for more time to file the return (usually to October 15). Does not extend time to pay any tax owed.

💡 Interview Tips – Skills Check

Narrate while you work

Say out loud (in the language they are using at that moment): “I’m sorting by Status so Pending items come first… now adding a simple IF formula for the Action column.” This shows confidence and communication skill.

Speed is secondary to accuracy

They prefer a correct result in 90 seconds over a fast but messy result. Check one or two cells after writing a formula.

Use the tools they mention

Have Google Sheets and Google Calendar already open and signed in. If they ask for Excel/Outlook, switch calmly. Knowing both is a plus.

Bilingual flow

Part of the call will be in Spanish. Practice describing the same action in both languages: “I’m applying a filter” / “Estoy aplicando un filtro”.

If you get stuck

Say: “I would normally use XLOOKUP for this. Let me do it with a simpler method that still gives the correct result.” Then deliver a correct answer. Honesty + working solution is better than silence.
